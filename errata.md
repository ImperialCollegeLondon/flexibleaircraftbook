This is the list of known typos in the published edition of Palacios & Cesnik's book:

1. Page 79: missing a square in $a_1$ in the flutter onset condition (below Eq. 3.15) and in Figure 3.4. Flutter occurs when $a_1^2= 4 a_0 a_2$.
2. Page 219: missing a minus sign in the (1,3) term of the matrix in Eq. 6.21, it should be $-2\tilde\omega_B\textbf{M}_{\rho\xi}$.
3. Page 222: missing a minus sign in the (1,3) term of the first matrix in Eq. 6.32, it should be $-2\tilde\omega_B\textbf{M}_{\rho\xi}$.
4. Page 224: missing a minus sign in the (1,3) term of the first matrix in Eq. 6.36, it should be $-2\tilde\omega_B\textbf{M}_{\rho\xi}$.
5. Page 389: Y axis in Figure 9.24 should be normalized by $b$ instead of $b/2$.
